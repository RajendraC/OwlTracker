APP NAME (11/30)
Owl Tracker

SHORT DESCRIPTION (74/80)
Spending from your bank & UPI SMS: budgets, reports, backups. No internet.

FULL DESCRIPTION (3351/4000)
Owl Tracker reads the bank, card and UPI SMS alerts already on your phone and turns them into a clear monthly spending dashboard with budgets and reports. Reading these SMS is the app's core feature, and everything happens on your phone: Owl Tracker has no internet permission.

HOW IT WORKS
• Allow SMS access once. Owl Tracker finds debit and credit alerts from banks, cards, wallets and UPI apps.
• Choose what to import: pick the banks and cards, how far back to go (3, 6 or 12 months), money in or out, and words to skip.
• Review the list and untick anything that isn't a real expense, then import.
• New alerts are picked up automatically when you open the app and every few hours.

SEE WHERE YOUR MONEY GOES
• Monthly dashboard with spending by category and your most recent transactions.
• Real app icons for over 80 popular Indian apps, such as Swiggy, Zomato, Uber and IndiGo, when they are installed on your phone.
• Search your activity by merchant, category or note.

REPORTS YOU CAN TAP THROUGH
• Look at this month, or the last 3, 6 or 12 months, compared with the period before.
• Spent, received and net, a day-by-day or month-by-month chart, a category breakdown, top merchants and your spending by day of the week.
• Tap a day, month, category, merchant or weekday and every chart narrows to it, with the matching payments listed.
• Short highlights, such as your biggest category and your busiest day.

BUDGETS THAT WARN YOU EARLY
• Set a monthly budget, overall and per category.
• A pace marker shows whether you are spending faster than the month allows.
• Clear over-budget alerts with the amount over and where it went.
• Start your budget month on your salary day if you like.

YOUR CATEGORIES, YOUR RULES
• 18 ready-made categories; add, rename, recolour or archive your own.
• Fix a category once and Owl Tracker can remember it for that merchant.
• Add cash expenses yourself.

BACK UP AND MOVE TO A NEW PHONE
• Save a password-protected backup file, or share it straight to your new phone, and restore it there in a few taps.
• Backups never contain SMS text, account or card numbers, UPI IDs or bank references.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No internet permission: Owl Tracker never sends your messages or spending anywhere. A backup leaves your phone only if you send it yourself.
• OTPs, promotions and messages from people are skipped and never stored.
• Data is kept in an encrypted database (AES-256) whose key is protected by Android Keystore.
• Optional fingerprint or screen-lock app lock, and screenshot blocking.
• No account, no ads, no analytics.
• Delete single transactions, delete by bank or by age, or reset everything at any time.
