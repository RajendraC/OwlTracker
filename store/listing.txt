APP NAME (28/30)
Owl Tracker: Expense Tracker

SHORT DESCRIPTION (76/80)
Offline expense tracker from bank & UPI SMS. No internet, no account needed.

FULL DESCRIPTION (3704/4000)
Owl Tracker is an expense tracker and budget planner that turns the bank, card and UPI SMS already on your phone into a clear picture of your spending. It works completely offline: the app has no internet permission, needs no account or user ID, and keeps your data only on your phone, where you are always in control of it.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No internet access: Owl Tracker has no internet permission, so your messages and spending can never leave your phone.
• No account and no user ID: no sign-up, no email, no password. Open the app and start.
• Your data stays on your phone, in an encrypted database (AES-256) whose key is held by Android Keystore.
• You are in full control: back up to your own file, delete single expenses, delete by bank or by age, or erase everything at any time.
• OTPs, promotions and messages from people are skipped and never stored.
• Optional fingerprint or screen-lock app lock, and screenshot blocking. No ads, no analytics.

AN EXPENSE TRACKER THAT FILLS ITSELF IN
• Allow SMS access once. Owl Tracker finds debit and credit alerts from banks, cards, wallets and UPI apps.
• Choose what to import: the banks and cards, how far back (3, 6 or 12 months), money in or out, and words to skip.
• Review the list, untick anything that isn't a real expense, and import.
• New alerts are picked up when you open the app and every few hours.
• Prefer not to share SMS? Add your expenses yourself in manual mode.

SEE WHERE YOUR MONEY GOES
• Spending sorted into groups such as Shopping, Food, Getting around, Home & Bills, Health and Kids, each with finer categories: Fashion & Clothing, Phones & Electronics, Food Delivery, Groceries, Daily Commute, Medicines and more.
• Tap a group to see its categories, a category to see its shops, and a shop to see every payment.
• Six months at a glance: each group's trend, against your budget.
• Paid by: what each card and bank account was used for, by its last 4 digits.
• Week by week, and a calendar of your month: tap a day for its payments.
• Real app icons for about 200 popular Indian apps, when they are installed.

BUDGETS THAT WARN YOU EARLY
• Set a budget for each group with sliders, or one total for the month.
• See how much is left, the safe amount to spend a day, and what's changing from month to month.
• Clear alerts when a group goes over, with where the money went.
• Start your budget month on your salary day if you like.

REPORTS YOU CAN TAP THROUGH
• This month, or the last 3, 6 or 12 months, compared with the period before.
• Income and expenses, a trend for each category, top merchants, spending by day of the week, and ways to save.
• Tap a day, month, category or merchant and every chart narrows to it.

AMAZON, SPLITS AND RULES
• Orders from stores that sell everything wait in their group until you say what they were.
• Split one payment across categories.
• Fix a category once and Owl Tracker can remember it; see and undo your rules any time.

BACK UP AND MOVE TO A NEW PHONE
• Save a password-protected backup file, or share it to your new phone, and restore it there in a few taps.
• Backups never contain SMS text, account or card numbers, UPI IDs or bank references.
