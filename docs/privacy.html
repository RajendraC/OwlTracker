Owl Tracker privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026 · App: Owl Tracker (com.owl.tracker)

In short

Owl Tracker turns your bank, card and UPI SMS alerts into a spending dashboard on your phone. It has no internet permission, so your messages and your spending never leave the phone. We do not collect, store on servers, sell or share any of your data.

What Owl Tracker reads

With your permission (Android's SMS permission, asked for only after an explanation screen), Owl Tracker reads SMS messages in your inbox to find bank, card, wallet and UPI transactions. For each message it reads only the sender, the text and the time. It does not read contacts, call logs, photos, location or any other data.

Merchant logos

To show a merchant's or bank's logo, Owl Tracker checks whether one of about 140 well-known apps (shopping, delivery, streaming, UPI and banking apps such as Swiggy, Netflix or YONO SBI) is installed, and if so shows that app's own icon. It can only see those listed apps, never the full list of apps on your phone, and nothing about them leaves your phone. When you choose an icon for a merchant yourself, the choice is limited to those same apps.

What it ignores

Messages from people (phone-number senders), OTPs and verification codes, promotional messages, reminders and failed payments are skipped in memory and never stored.

What it keeps, and where

Only the transactions you choose to import, each with a copy of its original message so you can check it, plus your categories, budgets and settings. Everything is stored on your phone in a database encrypted with AES-256, whose key is protected by Android Keystore and never leaves the phone. Your data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

What it shares

Nothing. Owl Tracker has no internet permission, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no third-party SDKs that send data. Because no data leaves your device, Google Play's Data safety section for Owl Tracker states that no data is collected or shared.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Choose which senders and transactions to import, and change that any time.
  • Delete single transactions, delete by age or by bank, or use Reset, which erases everything the app stored and destroys its encryption key.
  • Remove SMS access any time in Android Settings → Apps → Owl Tracker → Permissions. The app keeps working with expenses you add yourself.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of its data. Your SMS inbox is never changed.

Backups

A backup file is made only when you ask for one (More → Backup & restore), and goes only where you send it: Owl Tracker never sends it anywhere itself. It holds amounts, dates, merchant names, the merchant names and icons you chose, categories, notes, budgets and category rules. It never holds SMS text, sender IDs, account or card numbers, UPI IDs or bank references, and long numbers inside merchant names are masked. With a password, the file is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and can't be read without it.

Payments

Owl Tracker is free for 7 days, then a one-time unlock bought through Google Play. Google Play takes the payment under Google's own privacy policy and terms. Owl Tracker never sees your card, UPI or bank details: the Play Store app on your phone only tells it whether the unlock has been bought. The free trial is counted on your phone. Owl Tracker sends nothing about the purchase anywhere.

Sample data

If you choose “Explore with sample data”, Owl Tracker imports 21 built-in example messages instead of reading your inbox. Reset removes them.

Children

Owl Tracker is meant for adults managing their own money and is not directed at children under 18.
